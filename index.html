Belief

What I think most brands get wrong.

Brands sell the product. I sell what it means to the customer.

The insight

A hundred products do the same thing. Meaning is what makes one of them a habit.

The method

On AI

02

My first 30 days

What I'd do with your account. Same playbook every time.

Week 1

Audit

  • Set the CAC and payback we can afford
  • Connect ad tracking to payments
  • Review campaigns and landing pages
  • Talk to customers and sales

Week 2

Focused tests

  • 3–5 customer problems as angles
  • Demos and new formats
  • Each test: a hypothesis, a cap, a metric

Week 3

Fix the bottleneck

  • No clicks → fix the creative
  • Clicks, no checkout → fix offer and onboarding
  • Pause the weak, build on the winners

Week 4

Scale

  • Fund what acquires at a sustainable cost
  • Report what works, where we lose people, what to fund next
→ then repeat

Not a one-off campaign. A loop that gets sharper every month.
